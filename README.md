# PhotoCraft Online

An independently hosted browser edition of the open-source PhotoCraft image editor, with English and Chinese guides.

**Website: [photocraftonline.org](https://photocraftonline.org/)**

- [Open the editor](https://photocraftonline.org/editor)
- [中文网站与教程](https://photocraftonline.org/zh/)
- [Beginner tutorial](https://photocraftonline.org/blog/photocraft-tutorial)
- [Editing tests and limitations](https://photocraftonline.org/blog/photocraft-review)

## What you can try

Basic editing is free and requires no account or installation. Open an image in your browser, crop it, add text, and download a PNG. You can also open a PSD to inspect its layers.

Start with a small file and keep the original. Imported images are processed in the browser, and the site has no cloud document storage. Download your work before closing or reloading the editor.

## Current limits

The hosted editor uses PhotoCraft v0.5.0, an early alpha release. Complex PSD compatibility needs checking with your own files. The site's small tests do not establish full Photoshop compatibility or reliable PSD save-and-reopen behavior. PNG exports flatten the layers.

The website and guides are available in English and Chinese; the editor retains its original interface. The site does not provide generative AI editing.

Read the [PSD compatibility guide](https://photocraftonline.org/blog/photocraft-psd-compatibility) before working with a layered document.
